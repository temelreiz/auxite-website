Emission model

AUXR is minted against reserve rather than pre-allocated. Tokens are issued as hedged basket exposure is added to the reserve; there is no pre-mined pool released on a schedule, and no inflation or emission rate.

MAX_SUPPLY is fixed in the contract at 1,000,000,000 AUXR. This is a hard ceiling enforced on-chain, not a planned issuance target — actual supply reflects reserve size and stands at 26,211 AUXR.

No lock-up or vesting. Neither the founder allocation nor the treasury holding is subject to a contractual lock-up, cliff or vesting schedule, and no such restriction is enforced on-chain. They are excluded from circulating supply because they are issuer-controlled, not because they are locked. The founder allocation is held to fund exchange listings and market development, and is expected to be distributed as that work proceeds; distribution will be reflected in updated circulating supply figures.
